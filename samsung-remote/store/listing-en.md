## 5. Category, tags and keywords

**Category: Tools (recommended)** over Lifestyle.
- Users searching for a TV remote look in Tools; it is where the large remote-control apps sit, so the app competes in the right charts.
- Lifestyle is a crowded catch-all (shopping, dating, recipes) and would bury a utility.
- Alternative if you ever want to stand out in a smaller chart: "Video Players & Editors" is *not* appropriate (the app plays no video) – stay with Tools.
