Privacy Policy – TV Remote

App: TV Remote (package com.dimslider.tvremote) · Effective date: 17 October 2026

In short: the app does not collect any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use any internet servers of ours. Commands go only from your phone directly to your own TV over your local Wi-Fi network.

1. Who we are

TV Remote is developed by an individual developer ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact us at [CONTACT_EMAIL].

2. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal or usage data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not send anything to the developer.

3. What the app does with data on your device

  • Communication with your TV. To find and control your TV, the app communicates on your local network (Wi-Fi). Button presses, text you type or dictate for the TV, and app-launch commands are sent directly from your phone to the TV you selected. They do not pass through any server of ours.
  • Stored locally. The app saves on your phone the list of TVs you added (name, network address and the pairing token or certificate the TV issues), your settings, shortcuts and recorded macros, and the trial/purchase status. This data never leaves your device through us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • Phone call state (optional). If you turn on auto-mute for calls, the app needs to know when a call starts ringing, is answered or ends, including internet calls such as WhatsApp. It does not read phone numbers, contacts, call logs or call content, and it records nothing.

4. Speech recognition

The app does not request microphone access. When you use a voice command, the app asks your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google's) to listen. That service is provided by your device's operating system under its own privacy terms; the app only receives the recognised text and uses it on your device or sends it to your TV.

5. Purchases

The app offers a free 7-day trial and a one-time purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google Play's handling of purchase information is covered by Google's privacy policy. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase was made.

7. Security

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side that could leak. Data stored on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

9. Your choices

You can turn off optional features (such as call auto-mute) and revoke permissions at any time in Android settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If a change ever affects how data is handled, it will also be noted in the app's release notes.
