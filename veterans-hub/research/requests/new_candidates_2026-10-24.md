# Azimut – new candidate services (scan of 2026-10-24)

Method: WebSearch only (standard mode, plus one extended query). I worked from result titles and snippets, because WebFetch and curl cannot reach the sites.
I checked every candidate against `existing.txt` (543 entries), grepping the Hebrew name, the English name and the domain. Known duplicates are listed at the end.
Privacy: organisation names and organisational contacts only. I left out the names of the private individuals who appeared in the articles.

Confidence scale: **high** = an official org or government page in the results states it; **medium** = a reputable news report with specific details; **low** = a headline or snippet only.

## A. Candidates (not in the directory)

### 1. מרפאת סטיבן א. כהן לוותיקים ומשפחות – מבואות החרמון (שיבא + Cohen Veterans Network)
- **What:** an outpatient trauma clinic. It offers individual and group therapy for PTSD, anxiety, depression, grief, sleep problems and couple/family issues, plus a dedicated support group for spouses of combat soldiers.
- **For:** IDF veterans, reservists and security personnel after 10/7, and their families.
- **Region:** North. Mevo'ot HaHermon Rehabilitation Campus, Koach Junction (צומת כ"ח), Upper Galilee.
- **Contact:** none in the snippet. Operated by Sheba, supported by FIDF and UJA-NY. Websites: cohenveteransnetwork.org and sheba-global.com.
- **Source:** https://www.jpost.com/health-and-wellness/mind-and-spirit/article-912027 ; also https://www.cohenveteransnetwork.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-CVN-Israel-Clinic-Press-Release_CVN_FIDF_UJA_FINAL.pdf
- **Seen:** 2026-10-24. Ribbon-cutting dated 14.10.2026.
- **Status:** active (newly opened). A satellite of the Modi'in clinic, which is already in the directory.
- **Confidence:** high/medium. The CVN press release is official, and the JPost report gives the opening.

### 2. מרכז להתערבות מוקדמת בטראומה – איכילוב (מרכז אסיף) וארגון "למענכם"
- **What:** a free center for early intervention in trauma. It offers short, focused treatment by clinical psychologists and clinical social workers. Complex cases are referred on to Ichilov. It has a dedicated track for family members: spouses, parents, siblings and children.
- **For:** reservists, security forces personnel and their families.
- **Region:** Tel Aviv. It operates from the offices of "Lema'anchem" and is managed by the Assif team.
- **Contact:** none in the snippet.
- **Source:** https://www.maariv.co.il/news/health/article-1357590 ; https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/bkek2wxdzl ; https://www.israelhayom.co.il/health/article/21232753
- **Seen:** 2026-10-24. The agreement was signed around August 2026.
- **Status:** unclear. An agreement has been signed, but no opening date was found.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Dedup note:** "מרכז אסיף" (Ichilov) is already in the directory. This is a new, separate free family/early-intervention arm with a different operator, so add it as its own entry or as a note on the Assif entry.

### 3. עוז ורוח (Oz VeRuach) – פורטלנד טראסט ישראל ומשרד הביטחון
- **What:** an employment program. Track one is AI and tech training, 4 days a week for 3 months, followed by job placement in hi-tech companies. The program also offers personal guidance and direct links to employers, and plans tracks outside hi-tech too.
- **For:** wounded IDF and security forces personnel, with an emphasis on wounded reservists.
- **Region:** national, with a stated focus on quality jobs in the periphery.
- **Contact:** none in the snippet. Run by Portland Trust Israel.
- **Source:** https://ejewishphilanthropy.com/portland-trust-pilots-program-to-move-wounded-idf-reservists-into-lucrative-tech-careers/ ; https://www.srugim.co.il/1305277-עוז-ורוח-שילוב-פצועי-צהל-בתעסוקה ; https://www.jns.org/feature/beyond-the-battlefield-israeli-initiatives-help-soldiers-transition-to-civilian-life
- **Seen:** 2026-10-24. The eJP report is about one month old.
- **Status:** active. The pilot cohort of 19 is done, and the program plans 160 participants in 2027 and 280 in 2028.
- **Confidence:** medium. Several outlets report it, but I found no official program page.
