## What is missing from the card

1. **status**: open for registration / closed / ongoing / ended, plus the date of the next cycle.
2. **eligibility_text**: one plain-language line saying who exactly is eligible ("מוכרים עם 20%+ על פוסט טראומה"), because the tags are too coarse.
3. **first_step**: one short first action (a call button / link to a form), separate from the long how_to_apply.
4. **response_time / what_happens_next**: what to expect after contacting them (a return call, an intake interview, a waiting list).
5. **hours**: opening hours as a separate field (now scattered through the description).
6. **gender / language / community**: men/women only, languages (Arabic, Russian), communities (Druze, religious). Now in free text only.
7. **accessibility**: physical accessibility and online/remote availability.
8. **verified_at** missing from some records (3, 11, 16, 17, 19, 22, 24, 28, 29, 35); every card should show "עודכן לאחרונה" and flag anything over 6 months old.
9. **crisis_footer**: a fixed emergency line on mental-health and crisis cards.
10. **official_source**: an indication of whether the source is official (gov/the organization itself) or secondary (news/an aggregator).
