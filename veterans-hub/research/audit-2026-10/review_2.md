## Recurring patterns and a rule for the whole database

1. **No phone on 24 of 36 records; on 8 of them (the 6 suppliers, Achim shel Yonatan, the policy document) there is no channel at all.**
   *Rule:* a record without `phone` will not be published unless `how_to_apply` names an exact channel (a form address, an app plus a screen, or a call center with hours). Shikum.Me suppliers: a phone and address are mandatory. Without them → `confidence: low` and hide from the default results.
2. **`cost` contradicts `cost_notes` (6 records:** CHOICE, Nature Heals, 18x, Kfar Sarah, Vertigo, TELA).
   *Rule:* if cost_notes contains "check / worth checking / not published / not detailed" → `cost` must be `unknown`. If it contains "co-payment / payment" → it cannot be `free`. An automatic check in the build.
3. **`cost: free` on monetary rights and loans** (records 0, 8, 13, 33). The field mixes "what it costs me" with "what I get".
   *Rule:* for `kind: money` add a value `cost: n/a` (or hide the field) and show the amount in a separate field.
4. **A secondary source in place of a primary one** (news articles in English, a fundraising page, a 2023 article): `confidence` is not lowered accordingly.
   *Rule:* a source_url that is not on the organization's domain or on gov.il → maximum `medium`. A source older than 12 months without `verified_at` → `low`.
5. **Absolute claims without a source:** "no queue", "fully subsidized", "12 sessions", "mental rescue".
   *Rule:* every number or absolute in the description needs a supporting source, or the attribution "according to the organization".
6. **Jargon and acronyms:** KABANs, compensation officer, welfare department in the district, tier, Tal"a, watsu, EMDR/CBT, battle shock casualties.
   *Rule:* a glossary that the app expands automatically on first appearance, plus a lint that fails on a list of forbidden acronyms in the description.
7. **Imprecise tagging:** `ptsd` on every gym, `sea` on a pool, `nature` on a benefits club, an empty `regions`, `police: yes` without `police` in eligibility.
   *Rule:* a `difficulties` tag only if the description explains the connection. `police: yes` ⇔ `police` ∈ eligibility (an automatic check).
8. **Long names (over 60 characters)** that break on a phone screen.
   *Rule:* a 45-character cap on `name`. The organization name goes into a separate field.

## What is missing on the card itself (proposed fields)

- **`hours`**: activity hours / opening days (today scattered in how_to_apply or missing, which is critical for a hotline and for "you just show up").
- **`org_name`**: the name of the operating organization separate from the program name (shortens `name`).
- **`wait_time`**: expected wait time (exists in free text only for ICA).
- **`next_step`**: one primary action button (phone / form / app) instead of free text.
- **`gender`**: men / women / mixed (today hidden in the description of the suppliers and of Metiv).
- **`age_range`**: (CHOICE 12–35, TELA up to 21, mentoring 5–18).
- **`delivery`**: in person / Zoom / phone.
- **`last_checked_by_human`** and the visible date "verified on…" on the card, so the reader knows how current it is.
- **`crisis_note`**: a fixed line on every mental-health card: "In acute distress: NATAL 1-800-363-363 or ERAN 1201", so a person who reaches a card with a 6-month wait is not left without a response.
- **`status`**: active / seasonal / ended / experimental (relevant for Hamakom, Metiv, Nature Heals, Belev Echad).
